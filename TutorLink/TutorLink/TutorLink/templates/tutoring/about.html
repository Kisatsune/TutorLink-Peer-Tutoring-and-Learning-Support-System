About TutorLink

Students who struggle with a subject often do not know who can help, and capable students have no organized way to volunteer. TutorLink brings both together: tutors post sessions, students book them, and an administrator keeps everything visible and fair.

Built on the TANGLAW values

1

KatapatanHonesty

Ratings and feedback are tied to real, attended bookings, and attendance is recorded by the tutor.

2

KahusayanExcellence

Tutors are approved by an administrator, and top-rated tutors are tracked.

3

MalasakitCare

Students help students; no one has to struggle with a subject alone.

4

PagkamalikhainCreativity

A peer-to-peer model that turns classmates into a learning community.

5

IntegridadIntegrity

Role-based access, token-secured admin tools, and no double-booking of slots.
